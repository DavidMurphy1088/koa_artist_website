About Koa

My name is Koa Joy Hood, I am an artist who works predominantly with paper, transforming furniture, nature's treasures and paper into one-of-a-kind artisan crafts and collage prints.

My journey began at eight years old, sitting cross-legged at the base of a peach blossom tree, sketching. Wide-eyed, surrounded by my nine siblings, sharing was woven into my DNA through my Māori roots, while growing up with scarcity taught me to find resourcefulness in necessity. Early on, I learned to see possibilities in the ordinary and magic in the mundane, as I first explored my creative desires.

Through up-cycling, decoupage, and multifunctional design, I’ve pieced together discarded fragments to reveal their humble, quiet beauty. As I have aged, I’ve walked gracefully (and not so gracefully) through these chapters of my life. Now in my sixties, I am reflecting on a lifetime's worth of continuous self discovery.

For years my creative energy was channelled entirely into guiding my children onto their paths. I’m now redirecting that same intense, instinctive devotion towards my own growth, which has been both challenging and profoundly fulfilling.

Each unique piece I create carries a bit of this story, a celebration of quiet beauty, second chances, and the simple joys of handmade art. I hope these creations bring warmth and light into your home.
