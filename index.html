Koa Joy Hood

Handcrafted decoupage art, original collage prints, and unique artisan crafts. Transforming furniture, nature's treasures, and paper into one-of-a-kind artisan crafts and collage prints. Paper is cut, torn and layered by hand. Layer by layer, paper becomes pattern, pattern becomes light.

About the Artist

My journey began at eight years old, sitting cross-legged at the base of a peach blossom tree, sketching. Through up-cycling, decoupage, and multifunctional design, I’ve pieced together discarded fragments to reveal their humble, quiet beauty.
